# Aaditude project brief

Recorded from the discovery interview and the Aaditude Instagram profile shared on 8 October 2026.

- **Store:** Aaditude, Bhopal
- **Customer:** Gen Z men, especially students and young working professionals
- **Assortment:** Initially described as trendy T-shirts and lowers; the Instagram feed also shows shirts, jackets, football jerseys and baggy jeans, which now appear as preview categories
- **Launch range:** About 10–20 styles, mostly ₹699–₹1,499; the website currently shows 11 provisional listings
- **Brand direction:** Clean and minimal site design, black and white, simple lettering; Instagram bio says “Luxury with attitude”
- **Reference sites:** No specific design examples
- **Pages:** Home, shop, product detail, bag, checkout, about, contact, size guide and FAQ
- **Photography:** Local copies of public photos from [@aaditude.vibe](https://www.instagram.com/aaditude.vibe/); original high-resolution product files have not been supplied
- **Payment:** UPI only
- **Order route:** WhatsApp order request from the checkout form
- **Missing before public launch:** Confirmed products, prices and stock; WhatsApp order number; UPI ID and QR; shipping and returns details; support contact

The photos reflect Aaditude's posts. The product names, prices and availability are provisional. Checkout remains disabled until the store settings and real catalogue are explicitly completed.
